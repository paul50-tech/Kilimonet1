What Smart Assist Will Deliver

Kenyan Crop Disease & Pest Scanner

Optical leaf-analysis engine calibrated for East African horticultural and staple crops, identifying fungal blights, bacterial wilts, nutrient deficiencies, and invasive pests with registered treatment plans.

  • Instant leaf photo examination
  • Kenyan registered chemical active ingredients & biologicals
  • Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI) & re-entry safety advice

Verified Agrovet Input Hub

A nationwide directory of certified agrodealers with stock availability verification, ensuring farmers access genuine crop protection chemicals, certified seeds, and specialized fertilizers.

  • County & sub-county location mapping
  • Direct WhatsApp inquiries and order dispatch
  • Genuine input assurance & manufacturer traceability

Connected Agronomist Dispatch

Direct 1-on-1 access to verified Kenyan agronomists and plant pathologists for clinical consultations, tailored spray schedules, and on-farm greenhouse inspections.

  • Fast clinical review for active crop distress
  • WhatsApp chat, phone advisory & farm visit options
  • Season-long crop calendar planning
