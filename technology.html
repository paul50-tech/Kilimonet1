Detailed Technology Capabilities

KiliOS™ — The Digital Brain of the Farm

Kilimonet's integrated farm management operating platform gives farm managers, agribusinesses, and cooperatives a unified command center for operations, resource planning, and financial transparency.

  • Crop & Livestock Records: Planting schedules, growth stages, treatments, and batch tracking.
  • Financial Tracking & Inventory: Real-time input cost tracking, harvest yields, sales, and stock ledger.
  • Workforce Management: Task assignment, attendance logs, and field worker performance metrics.

Built for African field realities with offline capability, multi-farm tenant switching, and custom executive reporting dashboards.

  • Automated farm activity calendars and SMS alerts
  • Client and investor reporting portals
  • Seamless sync with field IoT sensors and weather stations
Deploy KiliOS™

KiliSense™ — Smart Farm Monitoring

Industrial-grade IoT telemetry and environmental sensor arrays that capture ground-truth data 24/7 across open fields, shaded structures, and greenhouse environments.

  • Soil Health Telemetry: Multi-depth volumetric soil moisture and root-zone temperature probes.
  • Climate Sensing: Ambient air temperature, relative humidity, vapor pressure deficit (VPD), and solar radiation.
  • Water Storage: Ultrasonic water tank level sensors and borehole discharge monitors.

Actionable intelligence delivered straight to farm supervisors with instant alerts when environmental thresholds deviate from optimal agronomic targets.

  • Configurable SMS and WhatsApp threshold alerts
  • Solar-powered nodes with resilient long-range cellular / LoRaWAN connectivity
  • Historical trend logs for micro-climate analysis
Install KiliSense™

KiliFlow™ — Intelligent Irrigation & Water Systems

Automated precision water and nutrient delivery that maximizes crop yields while cutting water and electricity usage by up to 45% compared to manual methods.

  • Dynamic Scheduling: Soil-moisture and evapotranspiration-driven automated irrigation cycles.
  • Solar Pump Control: Smart variable-frequency drives (VFD) optimized for solar irradiance curves.
  • Flow & Pressure Monitoring: Real-time pipe burst detection, line blockage alerts, and consumption metering.

Engineered for high-value horticultural systems, drip tape networks, center pivots, and controlled-environment fertigation injectors.

  • Automated multi-channel fertilizer dosing and pH / EC balancing
  • Remote valve actuation via smartphone or cloud scheduler
  • Comprehensive water audit and conservation compliance logs

KiliVision™ & KiliDrone™ — Remote Oversight & Precision Aerial Analytics

KiliVision™: Multi-sensor surveillance, remote IP camera streaming, and computer vision algorithms that detect early pest manifestations and safeguard farm assets.

  • 24/7 perimeter protection and gate automation logging
  • Time-lapse visual crop development records
  • AI canopy vigor indexing and anomaly flagging

KiliDrone™: Certified drone pilots operating multispectral cameras to deliver accurate agronomic cartography across expansive acreages.

  • High-resolution orthomosaic 2D/3D elevation and slope maps
  • NDVI & NDRE vegetative health index heatmaps for targeted spraying
  • Stand count validation and rapid crop damage assessments

KiliLearn™, KiliMarket™ & KiliCarbon™ — Capacity, Commerce & Climate

KiliLearn™: Practical, field-tested capacity building delivered through on-site demonstration hubs, interactive digital modules, and agronomist-led grower masterclasses.

KiliMarket™: Direct commercial pipelines linking verified farmers and cooperatives with institutional off-takers, export aggregators, and food processors.

KiliCarbon™: Climate resilience advisory helping farms measure soil carbon sequestration, qualify for sustainability certifications, implement agroforestry, and adopt regenerative farming practices.

  • Baseline soil organic carbon (SOC) audits and testing
  • Verified regenerative agriculture conversion roadmaps
  • Global GAP & fair-trade certification preparation

Controlled-Environment Agriculture

Advanced Soil-Less Growing Technologies

Hydroponics

Nutrient Film Technique (NFT) and Dutch Bucket substrate systems designed for high-density production of leafy greens, tomatoes, peppers, and strawberries with 90% less water than conventional soil farming.

Aeroponics

Precision root-misting chambers providing oxygen-rich nutrient fog for rapid rooting, virus-free potato seed propagation, and accelerated harvest cycles in sterile environments.

Aquaponics

Circular bio-integrated ecosystems pairing freshwater aquaculture (Tilapia/Catfish) with organic crop beds, utilizing natural nitrifying bacteria to convert fish waste into plant nutrients.

Smart Climate Greenhouses

Poly-tunnel and polycarbonate structures fitted with automated shade screens, roll-up ventilation motors, evaporative cooling pads, and fogging systems for climate regulation.
