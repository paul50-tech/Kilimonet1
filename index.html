Connected Platform

The Kilimonet Integrated Agrisystems Ecosystem (KIAE)

Modern agriculture is a connected ecosystem where data, technology, infrastructure, finance, knowledge, and people work together. KIAE links six interconnected domains under a single accountable partner:

1

Farm Design & Development

Master planning, surveying, soil testing & BOQs.

2

Smart Production

Crops, livestock & regenerative agronomy.

3

Digital Agriculture

Digitising farm operations & decision support.

4

Smart Infrastructure

Greenhouses, smart irrigation & solar pumping.

5

Market & Value Chain

Buyer linkages, quality standards & traceability.

6

Knowledge & Innovation

Farmer field schools, demonstration & research.

Guided by the Kilimonet Implementation Framework™ (KIF)

5-Phase structured delivery: 1. Discover • 2. Analyse • 3. Design • 4. Implement • 5. Optimise
